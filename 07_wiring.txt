WIRING

ESP32 DevKit V1 / WROOM-32
Servo:
  Servo signal  -> GPIO 18
  Servo VCC     -> 3.3V
  Servo GND     -> GND

Onboard LED:
  GPIO 2 -> onboard LED (normally already connected)
  No external LED wiring required.

USB:
  ESP32 USB -> laptop

SERVO POWER:
Do NOT power a larger servo from the ESP32 3.3V pin.
For a small SG90, a separate regulated 5V supply is recommended.
